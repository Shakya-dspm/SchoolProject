History

Since 2004, Dr. Niroth Samarawickrama has been at the forefront of reshaping software engineering education, empowering students with the expertise required to succeed in the tech industry. Under his leadership, many students are now in senior positions at leading global software companies, while others have founded successful businesses. Dr. Samarawickrama’s unwavering focus on innovation, hands-on learning, and industry alignment continues to contribute significantly to the growth and development of Sri Lanka’s IT sector.
